# Pokerogue assets used by Zenpho

Animation data, cries, and battle sound effects were copied from
[pagefaultgames/pokerogue-assets](https://github.com/pagefaultgames/pokerogue-assets)
at commit `009cac30c39d3615c7b2c320c3984af368871031` (beta branch).

- The 151 supported Pokémon use PokéRogue's animated sprite atlases in
  `pokemon/`. Each PNG atlas and its generated JSON frame map are loaded locally
  by Zenpho. These sprite files were downloaded from the upstream beta branch on
  2026-10-20; the JSON atlases are generated by PokéRogue tooling. Consult the
  upstream [image asset notices](https://github.com/pagefaultgames/pokerogue-assets/blob/beta/images/REUSE.toml)
  for the per-asset credits and license metadata.

- The 28 move animation JSON files are identified upstream as
  `AGPL-3.0-only`, copyright 2024-2026 Pagefault Games.
- The battle animation sheets are from the same asset repository; see its
  [image asset notices](https://github.com/pagefaultgames/pokerogue-assets/blob/beta/images/REUSE.toml).
- The grass battle backdrop and platforms in `images/arenas/` are from the same
  repository and are listed in the local REUSE manifest.
- Pokemon cries, wild battle/victory music, and general battle sound effects are identified upstream as
  `LicenseRef-FAIR-USE`, attributed to Nintendo, Creatures Inc., and GAME FREAK
  inc. The upstream repository says it does not claim ownership or relicense
  those assets. See its
  [audio asset notices](https://github.com/pagefaultgames/pokerogue-assets/blob/beta/audio/REUSE.toml)
  and [fair-use notice](https://github.com/pagefaultgames/pokerogue-assets/blob/beta/LICENSES/LicenseRef-FAIR-USE.txt).
- `PRSFX-*.wav` move cues are attributed upstream to The Pokemon Reborn Team
  under `LicenseRef-POKEMON-REBORN`. Pokemon Reborn's
  [borrowing guidance](https://www.rebornevo.com/pr/gamefaq/#borrow) requests
  credit for borrowed assets; this attribution and source project link are
  provided here.

The upstream AGPL and fair-use notices, plus the Pokemon Reborn attributed
notice, are included in `LICENSES/` beside these assets.

Zenpho's battle renderer adapts the supplied animation frames to its own arena
coordinates and keeps the existing palette and background. PokéRogue Pokémon
sprites are used in battle, collection, and practice views. Pokémon and source
project names remain the property of their respective owners. Zenpho is not
affiliated with or endorsed by these projects or Nintendo.
